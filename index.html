Ketabi Project

"Ketabi" Project

A Platform for Selling and Exchanging Used University Books

The Problem

  • The significant and continuous rise in the prices of university books and references.
  • Students' temporary need for books (only one semester).
  • Accumulation of old books with senior students without the ability to benefit from them.
Discussion Notes:

"Simply put, Professor, as students, we pay a lot of money for books we use for only one semester. Then they are left at home, and we don't know who to sell them to in order to benefit from them again."

The Solution (The Idea)

Designing a website that brings students of the same university together on one platform.
Facilitating the process of selling and exchanging used textbooks at highly discounted prices.

The Result: Saving money for the buying student and generating financial return for the selling student.

Discussion Notes:

"Our solution is similar to platforms like 'OLX' or 'Souq', but dedicated to university books. Students sell to each other; the buyer saves, and the seller earns."

Do people want it?

Ability to execute

Can we do it?

Viability

Profitability and returns

Will it make money?

Business Model Canvas (Ketabi)

Customer Segments

  • Buying students.
  • Selling students.

Who will use it? All university students across different years.

Customer Relationships

  • Self-service.
  • Reviews & Ratings.

Students interact directly. Reviews build trust.

Channels

  • Website & student groups.

How do we reach them? Social media and college ads.

Value Proposition

For Buyer

Saving time and money.

For Seller

Quick financial return.

The buyer saves, the seller earns. Mutual benefit.

Key Activities

  • Platform management.
  • Marketing to students.

What is our role? Running and marketing the site.

Key Resources

  • The Website.
  • Student database.

What do we own? The site itself and data.

Key Partnerships

  • Student Union.
  • Delivery agents.

Who helps us? Student Union will spread the idea.

Cost Structure

  • Hosting.
  • Printing ads.

What do we pay for? Domain and flyers.

Revenue Streams

  • 5% sales commission.
  • Ads for bookstores.

How do we earn? Sales percentage and bookstore ads.
